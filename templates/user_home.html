Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At Wellness Wave, we prioritize your data privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your information.

1. Data Collection

We collect health-related metrics (BMI, BP, Blood Sugar, etc.) provided by you to perform disease risk analysis. Personal identifiers like email and name are used for account management.

2. Use of Information

Your data is used solely to generate health risk reports and connect you with qualified medical professionals. We do not sell your personal health information to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption to protect your sensitive health data. Access to medical records is restricted to you and the doctors you explicitly consult with through our platform.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preferences.
